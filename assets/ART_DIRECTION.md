# PULSE 0.2.0 art provenance

The selected direction is the bright mineral valley (proposal B), revised to reserve most of the image for playable space. All art is original to this project.

- `assets/world/mineral_valley.jpg`: generated with the built-in image tool, then JPEG-encoded for efficient packaging. Original generation was a landscape 16:9 near-vertical orthographic mineral valley with 85% quiet open sandy terrain; warm ivory, peach/coral sandstone, muted sage scrub and violet crystals only around the outermost edges. No baked road, buildings, slots, units, UI or text. Actual route paving is generated by BoardView from the unchanged level geometry.
- `assets/vector/*.svg`: original layered vector art authored in `tools/generate_vector_art.py`. Tower base and turret are separate assets; the enemy body is separate from its six articulated legs. Core and portal are separate assets. Re-run the generator to reproduce SVGs.
- Greek typography uses the existing DejaVu Sans regular/bold font assets and their existing license.

## Terrain generation prompt

Original game production asset, a seamless-looking open mineral valley battlefield terrain plate for PULSE mobile tower defense. Landscape 16:9, 1280x720 or larger. Strict orthographic TOP DOWN near vertical view, NO horizon, NO UI, NO text, NO units, NO towers, NO roads, NO grid, NO circles, NO foundations. Bright polished hand-painted sculpted mineral world palette warm sand ivory ground, peach coral sandstone, muted sage green scrub and subtle violet crystals. Essential: 85% of entire image must be OPEN flat quiet playable sand terrain, smooth broad readable surface, no deep ravines or giant rocks inside battlefield. Small artistically arranged clumps of faceted coral rock, ivory strata, tiny purple crystals and dusty green succulents only at the OUTERMOST 5% edges, especially corners. Center and all inner parts very open, delicate sand ripples and sparse small flat mineral facets, subtle texture, bright yet not washed out, soft shadows consistent from upper left. Avoid noisy speckles. Mobile game polished stylized illustration with broad clear forms and subtle material richness, low angular extruded edges at bottom outer border only. This terrain will receive actual road paving, physical build sites, 28 compact turrets, animated enemies and particles in the engine; don't draw those yourself. No water, no scenery occupying central area. Prioritize exceptionally generous usable battle space.
